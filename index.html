The Invitations of Jesus

Every “come” He ever spoke, gathered in one place

Over and over, in every season of His ministry and even from the throne, Jesus’s word to people is one word — come. He said it to the weary and the curious, to fishermen at their nets and to a church at its door. He has never stopped saying it. Here are His invitations, in His own words.

✦

“Come to me, all you who labor”

“Come to me, all you who labor and are heavily burdened, and I will give you rest. Take my yoke upon you, and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart; and you will find rest for your souls. For my yoke is easy, and my burden is light.”

Matthew 11:28–30

For the weary: if you are carrying more than you can hold, rest is not a wage you earn at the end of the work — Jesus gives it freely, to anyone who simply comes.

“I will in no way cast out”

“All those whom the Father gives me will come to me. He who comes to me I will in no way cast out.”

John 6:37

For the one afraid of being turned away: perhaps you fear you have come too late, or too often, or too broken. Jesus has never cast out a single person who came to Him — and He will not begin with you.

“If anyone is thirsty”

“If anyone is thirsty, let him come to me and drink! He who believes in me, as the Scripture has said, from within him will flow rivers of living water.”

John 7:37–38

For the thirsty: if you have drunk from every other well and still wake up empty, this is spoken to you. Come and drink — and find the thirst itself answered, until life flows out of you to others.

“Come, and you will see”

He said to them, “Come, and see.” They came and saw where he was staying, and they stayed with him that day. It was about the tenth hour.

John 1:39

For the curious and the not-yet-sure: two men asked Jesus where He was staying, and He did not hand them an argument — He opened His door. You do not have to understand everything first. Come and see, and stay awhile.

“Come after me”

“Come after me, and I will make you fishers for men.”

Matthew 4:19

For ordinary people at ordinary work: Jesus found fishermen mending nets, not saints in a sanctuary. His call is not only to believe something about Him, but to follow Him — and He Himself makes your life fruitful as you go.

“I stand at the door and knock”

“Behold, I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears my voice and opens the door, I will come in to him, and will dine with him, and he with me.”

Revelation 3:20

For the one who hears a knock: He does not break the door down. He stands and knocks and waits to be welcomed — not to scold you, but to sit at your table and share a meal, as a friend.

The last invitation in the Bible

“The Spirit and the bride say, ‘Come!’ He who hears, let him say, ‘Come!’ He who is thirsty, let him come. He who desires, let him take the water of life freely.”

Revelation 22:17

For anyone at all: this is the final word heaven speaks to the world in all of Scripture, and it is not a warning — it is an invitation. No price is named, because there is none. The water of life is free.

✦

An open door, and one word

The Bible ends the way Jesus began: with an open door and one word. From the shore of Galilee to the throne room of heaven, through His whole ministry and on the very last page of Scripture, He never stopped saying it — and He has not stopped now. Whoever you are, and whatever you are carrying, the invitation still stands, and it is spoken to you.

Come.
